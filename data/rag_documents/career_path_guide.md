# Civil Service Career Pathways & Competency Development Roadmap

## Career Trajectory Framework
Career progression in the prototype is structured around competency milestones rather than mere seniority.
Target roles require higher proficiency in specific behavioral, functional, and digital competencies.

## Key Progression Pathways
1. Section Officer (R001) -> Project Officer (R002)
   - Core development focus: Project Management (Level 2 -> 4), Decision Making (Level 3 -> 4), Digital Governance (Level 2 -> 4).
   - Typical preparation timeline: 12 months.
   - Key milestone activities: Lead a departmental digitization pilot; pass Project Management Essentials; complete 3 mock interviews.

2. Section Officer (R001) -> Under Secretary (R003)
   - Core development focus: Government Drafting (Level 4 -> 5), Decision Making (Level 3 -> 4), Public Procurement & Financial Rules (Level 3 -> 4).
   - Typical preparation timeline: 24 months.
   - Key milestone activities: Master Cabinet Note drafting; complete Advanced GFR certification; handle parliamentary committee briefings.

3. Project Officer (R002) -> Digital Governance Lead (R004)
   - Core development focus: Digital Governance & Data Literacy (Level 4 -> 5), Citizen Centricity (Level 3 -> 4).
   - Typical preparation timeline: 18 months.
   - Key milestone activities: Implement cross-departmental API data sharing; oversee cyber audit compliance; design omnichannel citizen feedback system.
