# Common Syllabus Framework for Civil Services Capacity Building

## Core Modules Overview
The Common Syllabus covers essential foundation areas for all public servants across central and state ministries:

### 1. Public Service & Ethics (SYL001)
- Foundations of constitutional governance, civil service values, impartiality, conflict-of-interest management, RTI Act transparency, and public trust.

### 2. Digital Government & Emerging Technologies (SYL002)
- India Stack fundamentals (Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, e-Office, GeM), data privacy under DPDP Act, cloud architecture principles, cybersecurity hygiene, and AI applications.

### 3. Administrative Noting, Drafting & Parliamentary Procedures (SYL003)
- CSMOP standards, concise file noting, drafting Office Memorandums, Cabinet Notes with inter-ministerial consensus, and answering Starred/Unstarred Parliamentary Questions.

### 4. Problem Solving & Analytical Decision Making (SYL004)
- Evidence-based policy analysis, data interpretation, root-cause diagnostics (5 Whys, Ishikawa), stakeholder trade-off matrices, and crisis management protocols.

### 5. General Financial Rules & Public Procurement (SYL005)
- GFR 2017 principles, GeM portal workflows (direct purchase, L1 bidding, custom bids, reverse auction), contract lifecycle management, budgeting, and audit preparedness.

### 6. Project Management & Mission Execution (SYL006)
- Government Mission-Mode project lifecycle, Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), Gantt chart scheduling, critical path monitoring, vendor SLA enforcement, and impact evaluation.
