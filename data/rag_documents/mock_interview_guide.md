# AI Mock Interview Guidance & STAR Evaluation Framework

## Interview Methodology
Mock interviews in AI Karmayogi assess readiness for target roles through situational, behavioral, and domain-specific scenarios.
Interviews emphasize structured thinking, adherence to governance norms, and practical problem-solving.

## The STAR Methodology for Behavioural Answers
Candidates are encouraged to answer using the STAR structure:
1. Situation: Briefly set the context, department, and constraints (15-20% of response).
2. Task: Clearly define the specific challenge, statutory requirement, or objective (15% of response).
3. Action: Detail the step-by-step actions taken, rules applied, stakeholders consulted, and decisions made (50% of response).
4. Result: Quantify outcomes, improvements in citizen service, compliance achievements, and key lessons learned (15-20% of response).

## Five-Dimensional Evaluation Scale (1 to 5 points)
1. Relevance: Did the response directly address the core dilemma and role requirements?
2. Clarity & Structure: Was the answer well-structured, coherent, and logically sequenced (STAR format)?
3. Reasoning & Depth: Did the candidate demonstrate root-cause understanding and evidence-based analysis?
4. Role Competency: Did the candidate exhibit the required competency level expected for the target position?
5. Practical Judgment & Ethics: Was the proposed action compliant with GFR/conduct rules, pragmatic, and ethically sound?

## Scoring Matrix
- 4.5 - 5.0: Outstanding (Exemplary readiness, strong leadership and precision)
- 3.5 - 4.4: Proficient (Meets role requirements, minor refinement in structure needed)
- 2.5 - 3.4: Developing (Moderate gaps in specific competencies or rule awareness)
- 1.0 - 2.4: Foundational / Needs Remediation (Significant development needed in core competencies)
