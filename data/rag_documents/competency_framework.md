# National Civil Service Competency Framework & Taxonomy

## Overview
The Competency Framework defines behavioural, functional, domain, and digital competencies required across civil service roles. Competencies are mapped on a 5-level proficiency scale:
- Level 1: Foundational (basic understanding, requires supervision)
- Level 2: Developing (independent in routine operations, applies standard rules)
- Level 3: Proficient (analyzes complex scenarios, handles ambiguity, guides peers)
- Level 4: Advanced (strategic design, resolves conflicting priorities, institutional leadership)
- Level 5: Expert (policy formulation, national guidance, systemic governance)

## Behavioural Competencies
### Decision Making (C001)
- Core definition: Sound discretionary judgment, objective evaluation of evidence, balancing public interest with regulatory compliance.
- Key indicators: Avoids analysis paralysis, structures risk-weighted trade-offs, adheres to constitutional fairness.

### Public Service Orientation & Ethics (C003)
- Core definition: Uncompromising integrity, dedication to public good, empathetic citizen interaction, zero tolerance for corruption.
- Key indicators: Proactive conflict-of-interest disclosure, adherence to CCS Conduct Rules 1964, RTI compliance.

## Functional Competencies
### Project Management (C002)
- Core definition: Planning, scheduling, resource allocation, risk mitigation, and milestone delivery for government schemes.
- Key indicators: Use of Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), Critical Path Method (CPM), vendor SLA monitoring, quarterly expenditure targets.

### Government Communication & Drafting (C004)
- Core definition: Precision file noting, cabinet notes preparation, inter-ministerial communication, parliamentary question responses.
- Key indicators: Observance of Central Secretariat Manual of Office Procedure (CSMOP), unambiguous drafting, objective synthesis of facts.

## Digital & Domain Competencies
### Digital Governance & Data Literacy (C005)
- Core definition: Adoption of e-Office, DigiLocker, India Stack, data protection principles (DPDP Act), cybersecurity hygiene.
- Key indicators: Evidence-based analytics, API interoperability, privacy by design, digital workflow optimization.

### Public Procurement & Financial Rules (C006)
- Core definition: Mastery of General Financial Rules (GFR 2017), GeM portal procurement, contract drafting, and CAG/internal audit compliance.
- Key indicators: Fair competition enforcement, bid evaluation transparency, avoidance of fiscal irregularities.
